Palm's discount to soybean oil widened from 25.7% in March to 36.6% by June, then froze

Discount = (palm − soy) / soy. The USD spread was widest in May at −645 USD/t; the percent of soy was widest from June at −36.6%. July–September repeats the June print. It is not a new market, and it is not the 30-day forecast.
